I am a Presidential Young Professorship Assistant Professor of Accounting at the NUS Business School, National University of Singapore.

I received my Ph.D. in Accounting from Cornell University in 2025. In my research, I use archival methods to examine issues related to financial accounting. My research interests lie in the dynamics of information transmission in markets and firms. Specifically, I examine the transmission of information among capital market participants, such as managers, infomediaries, and investors, as well as the transmission of information among the workforce within firms.
